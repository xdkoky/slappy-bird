Privacy Policy

Slappy Fih is a free game made by one independent developer (“xdkoky”, “we”). This page explains what the game stores about you, why, and how to get it deleted. The short version: the game only keeps what it needs to run the leaderboard, friends, private chat between friends and races. On slappyfih.com you can choose to watch a video ad for an in-game reward; ads never play unless you choose one, and they are provided by Google (see Ads). There is no analytics, and we never sell your data.

What we don't collect

We don't ask for your real name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or payment details. There are no sign-in accounts with other companies and no third-party analytics. The only third party that may use cookies or advertising identifiers is Google, for the optional ads described below.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (best score, coins, tokens, unlocked looks, settings) is saved on your phone or computer. So is a random player ID and a random secret, which prove to our server that you are you, and a count of how many reward ads you watched today (for the daily limit). This count stays on your device.

Ads

On slappyfih.com the game can offer a short video ad in exchange for an in-game reward, for example a second chance or double coins. Ads only ever play when you choose to watch one; nothing pops up by itself, and there is a daily limit. If you skip an ad, you simply don't get the reward.

The ads are provided by Google AdSense (Google Ireland Limited in the EU). To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and may receive information such as your IP address and device details. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland you are asked first whether you agree to personalised ads; if you say no, Google shows non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time, and manage Google's ads settings at adssettings.google.com. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We never see or receive this advertising data ourselves.

Who can see it

Other players can see your nickname, your scores and your look. Chats are only between friends, and can only be read by the two friends in them. The developer can see everything above to run the game, deal with reports and help with lost accounts.

Where it is kept

The database is hosted by Supabase, the optional ads come from Google, and the website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website host, they may log technical data such as IP addresses to keep their services running. If you turn on reminders, they are delivered through your browser's push service (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android), which only sees the encrypted message. The App Store app is distributed by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Chat messages are deleted after 30 days. Reminder data is deleted when you turn reminders off or remove the app. Everything else is kept until you delete your account. Transfer codes expire after 10 minutes.

Deleting your account

In the game, open Settings → Account → Delete and type DELETE. This removes your player, scores, friends, messages, backups and everything else above from our server straight away, and resets the game on that device. It can't be undone. If you can't open the game any more, contact us (below) with your nickname and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, the UK or California), you have the right to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object to how it's used. We use your data only to provide the game you asked to play (and, if you choose to watch ads, Google uses data as described under Ads). To use any of these rights, contact us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child under 13 has used the online features, contact us and we'll delete their account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and mention it in the game's patch notes.

Chat rules

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive players. In the chat and in nicknames:

  • No bullying, harassment, hate speech or threats
  • No sexual or violent content
  • No sharing personal information (yours or anyone else's)
  • No spam, scams or advertising

A word filter hides the worst words. Tap any message to report it or to block its sender. Blocking hides that player's messages and stops them from messaging you or adding you as a friend. Every report is reviewed by the developer, usually within 24 hours. A player reported by several people is muted automatically until the report has been reviewed, and players who break the rules are muted or removed.
